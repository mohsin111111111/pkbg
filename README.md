PKBG

PKBG is a commando based action game where the player has to fight through enemy bases and save his fellow mates.

# Story

The player starts in an enemy base and has to cross the fence and eliminate 3 enemies. One of the enemies has a secret key which is required to open the locked door at the end of the area.

After entering the next room the player has to find a way across the wall by jumping on crates and reaching the other side.

The next area has CCTV cameras. If the camera detects the player the guard on the tower will shoot the player. The player has to destroy the cameras and move forward.

The player then reaches a computer lab. Find the working computer and press E to interact with it. Solve the puzzle using the sequence 2 3 1 to open the next door.

After this the player encounters different guards which can duck while shooting. Clear the area and reach the tower.

Inside the tower the player has to climb the platforms and find the fuse boxes. Shoot the fuse boxes to restore power to the lift.

Use the lift to reach the top and use the sniper to eliminate the far enemy.

After that the player has to perform a leap of faith and land on the net. Missing the net causes fall damage and restarts the level.

The player then reaches an area with gatekeeping guards. Use G to throw rocks and distract them. Use C to crouch and sneak inside the hall.

A large red guard is inside the hall. Sneak behind him and use K to perform a stealth knife kill.

Press E near the hostages to free them.

Continue through the hall using stealth kills or the sniper and eliminate the guards near the doors. Destroy the cameras and reach the bunker.

Use V to deploy the RC car and drive it through the gap into the bunker. Find the fuse box and destroy it using X. The bunker door will then open.

The player reaches a rope way. Press E to use the rope and descend safely. Jumping from the platform causes fall damage.

The player then enters the underground tunnels and fights through the enemies.

At the dead end break the glass on the right side to start the final fight. Eliminate the enemies and save the remaining 3 guards.

Find the escape route and move through the difficult tunnel until reaching the stairs. Use W and Space to climb up and escape.

After escaping the player has successfully completed the mission and saved all the hostages.

This is only the beginning of the story. More will come in Part 2.

4	M416
Left Mouse Button	Shoot
Right Mouse Button	Aim and Scope
E	Interact
K	Knife Stealth Kill
G	Throw Rock
V	Deploy RC Car
X	Destroy Fuse Box
